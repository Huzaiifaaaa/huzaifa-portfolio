I'm Muhammad Huzaifa, a full stack software engineer living in Oulu, Finland. I specialise in payment infrastructure and security-critical systems: backend-heavy software that has to be correct, fast and secure. I have 3+ years of production experience across FinTech and DefTech, and I'm studying for an MSc in Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Oulu, specialising in cyber security.

This blog is where I'll write about the things I build, the problems I run into, and what I learn along the way. This first post is the short version of how I got here.

## What I do

Most of my work sits where software engineering meets security. Day to day that means:

- **Backend and distributed systems**: Java and Spring Boot, C# and ASP.NET Core, Go and Python, with PostgreSQL, Kafka and RabbitMQ behind them.
- **Full stack product work**: React on the front, REST APIs in the middle, and owning a feature from the database schema to the deploy.
- **Delivery and infrastructure**: Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins and GitHub Actions on Azure and Google Cloud, with zero-downtime releases as the goal.
- **Security and compliance**: PCI-DSS, GDPR, the OWASP Top 10 and SIEM-driven monitoring, treated as engineering requirements rather than paperwork.

## What I've been doing

### Defence systems at Rapidev (2022 to 2024)

I started as a part-time trainee engineer at Rapidev in Islamabad while finishing my degree. I worked on RapiGuard, an anti-drone system, integrating detectors, jammers and spoofers over GraphQL so threats could be detected in real time, and building REST APIs so it could talk to other defence software. I also architected a key management application that injects cryptographic keys into radio hardware over UART, which was deployed across military communications systems in the Gulf region, and built a Flutter app that gives field operators live location tracking on a map. It taught me early that some software has to work every single time.

### Open source on Apache AGE (2023)

From March to August 2023 I interned with Bitnine, contributing to Apache AGE, the graph database extension for PostgreSQL, and building the AG Cloud website. It was my first real experience of working in the open, with code reviewed by people I'd never met.

### OptiGuard and my BSc at NUST (2020 to 2024)

I studied Computer Science at the National University of Sciences & Technology. My thesis, OptiGuard, is an attention-driven, explainable deep learning model for glaucoma classification. It reached state-of-the-art results on the G-1020 and SMDG datasets, was awarded the 1st Best Project distinction by industry judges, and was [published at IEEE EMBC 2025](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11253669) in Copenhagen.

### Payment infrastructure at Datapulse (2024 to 2026)

For just over two years I was a full stack engineer at Datapulse Technologies, working remotely for a team in Ireland on a multi-region whitelabel payment gateway. The biggest piece was replacing a legacy gateway with a new platform that now serves more than 1,000 merchants. Along the way I:

- found a 25% performance gain in the legacy architecture,
- integrated three global acquirers plus Apple Pay and Google Pay,
- merged separate payment systems into one gateway,
- owned the CI/CD pipeline from GitHub through Jenkins and Docker to Kubernetes,
- led PCI-DSS readiness, closing more than 15 audit findings before certification,
- and stood in as lead for a four-person team.

### Nafalytics (side project)

Nafalytics, from *nafa* (profit) and analytics, is a stock portfolio tracker I built on my own for the Pakistani market, where good tools were missing. I took it from idea to launch in three months: real-time profit and loss, FIFO trade matching, watchlists and notifications. It now has around 300 active users. Doing design, database, backend, frontend and cloud deployment alone taught me more about trade-offs than any single job.

## What I'm doing now

In September 2025 I started my master's at the University of Oulu. From June to August 2026 I was a research intern there, extending the [AVISE](https://github.com/ouspg/AVISE) security evaluation framework to text-to-image AI models. I designed a 16-case adversarial test suite and built connectors to evaluate models from 4+ providers, including Google, xAI, OpenAI and open-source diffusion models, in a reproducible way. I co-authored a paper on the work, which has been submitted to NordSec 2026.
